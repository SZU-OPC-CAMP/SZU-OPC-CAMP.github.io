OPC + Agent AI Native Startup Workshop

A project-based bootcamp entry point for generating, filtering, and incubating AI-native talent and early-stage projects. We help students, industry professionals, and entrepreneurs master Agent-driven workflows, form small teams, and close the loop from idea to MVP and from capability to commercialization in real business scenarios. Not a typical training program, nor an incubator that waits for projects to mature — we start from Day 0.

Project-Based Learning Model

Each bootcamp starts with real business scenarios and assembles small teams around a deliverable project goal. Enterprise partners propose real problems; students from mixed disciplines form teams; under the guidance of lead instructors, industry mentors, and teaching assistants, they use Agent workflows to complete requirement interviews, solution design, MVP development, and Demo Day presentations.

01

Enterprise Partners

Enterprises or industry mentors propose real business problems, define verifiable delivery goals, and provide scenario data and user feedback channels.

02

Cross-Disciplinary Students

Students from technology, product, design, business, and other backgrounds mix into teams, complementing each other's skill gaps through real collaboration.

03

Lead Instructors & Industry Mentors

University mentors build the curriculum framework and methodology; frontline engineers, entrepreneurs, and investors serve as hands-on mentors, giving students feedback in real business contexts.

04

TAs & Agent Toolchain

Outstanding alumni serve as teaching assistants, together with Agent-assisted research, code generation, content production, and project management to boost team efficiency.

Mentor Team

The OPC mentor system combines university faculty and industry mentors. The Shenzhen University PLAN Lab faculty team is responsible for curriculum framework, methodology, and academic guidance; we also invite frontline engineers, entrepreneurs, and investors to serve as hands-on mentors, so students receive feedback in real business scenarios. Coverage spans LLM Agents, blockchain, wireless communications, social network data mining, and IoT.

Bootcamp Mechanism

OPC is neither a typical training course nor an incubator that waits for projects to mature. We start with real problems, cross-disciplinary teams, and Agent workflows, advancing through biweekly mentor reviews so that training, filtering, and incubation happen along the same project path.

Training

Training Approach

Project-based training at the core, with mentor reviews and progress checks every two weeks, advancing around Agent toolchains, real-scenario requirements, team collaboration, and Demo delivery.

Selection

Selection Approach

We collect candidates through the interest community and application form, then filter for direction interest, execution motivation, academic background, and team fit to form small project-based teams.
